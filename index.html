🌈 Gesture Afterlife — leave moving color inside the volume

Touch motions write actual pigment into the persistent 3D volume, then existing flow, fibers and folds stretch that color long after your hand leaves. Prism, Staccato, Aurora and Ghost select combinations of the current three palette colors—not hardcoded RGB overlays. Choose Off to keep your old Stir behavior. One stroke = one Undo. 👻 Phantom Echoes — delayed moving offspring

Every few centimeters of your primary gesture may hatch two or three delayed offspring. They wander independently through real 3D space, depositing palette-linked pigment while existing forces carry their residue away. At most 12 live descendants and 6 newborn dabs per simulation tick; your own brush always has priority. Undo restores both pigment and unhatched offspring; Restart clears them.

⟡ Flow Necromancer — persistent force anchors

Tap artwork in Sculpt mode to place a force, or drag a glowing handle to reposition it. Each anchor persists and alters the actual 3D pigment and fiber flow. Six maximum to keep iPhone performance sane. Undo/Redo saves positions and their effects; Restart and New Design clear placed anchors.

⌁ Curve Witch — puppet the actual 3D forces

Grab a glowing strand between its control dots to bend the local force geometry; Reach controls how much of its length follows, Tension controls how hard it bends. Choose Translate to move the entire strand, or Edit Control Points to precisely drag an individual dot. Two fingers still twist and pinch the selected strand. All edits affect real pigment advection and save as one Undo step per gesture.

〰 Living Braid — the geometry itself moves

These options move the real 3D force paths once per simulation step. Writhe bends a path; Grow/Retract animates its tip; Unbraid/Rebraid and Tail Exchange need two paths. Tail Exchange brings two curves together and swaps their far ends; bake a pose to make it permanent. Baking is Undoable and never overwrites your pigment or fiber-memory history. Select Still to disable geometry motion without resetting anything.

🧬 Gene Thief — three-parent curve nursery

Capture the current editable curve system into a parent slot. Each parent remembers its 3D path(s), Stream/Coil/Gather forces, and Living Braid motion. The nursery stores just mathematical DNA, not your pigment. Capture again to overwrite that slot.
